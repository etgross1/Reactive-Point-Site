Who We Are

Engineering led by Eric Gross, P.E.

Eric T. Gross is a licensed Professional Engineer in North Carolina and South Carolina, with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from NC State University, where he focused on power systems. He gained early experience as a Transmission Protection & Controls intern at Duke Energy, then worked in the transportation industry designing and commissioning traction power substations for electric trains.

Eric has six years of experience as an electrical engineer, most recently as a Project Manager at an engineering consulting firm, leading utility and industrial substation design across North and South Carolina. His project work spans short circuit, protective coordination, and arc flash hazard studies; control panel layouts and specifications; AC/DC elementary drawings and wiring diagrams; protective relay settings; ground grid design; and standby generator systems.

He has also worked directly inside a client's operation as a contracted facility engineer — managing projects from design through construction and commissioning, and writing the switching procedures used on-site. That hands-on, embedded approach is exactly what Reactive Point now offers as Staff Augmentation.
